How the work would start

  1. Understand the process. Goal, people, available systems, data, constraints and the problem to solve.
  2. Define the delivery. Responsibilities, access, deliverables, tests and acceptance criteria before agreeing the scope.
  3. Build and check. Review progress and technical decisions, validate agreed scenarios and manage changes.
  4. Hand over and plan continuity. Documentation, delivery of artifacts and a support or evolution agreement appropriate to the engagement.

What to agree before an engagement

Responsibility and time commitment

Who prioritises, what each party owns, how progress is reviewed and what time overlap the team needs.

Data and systems

Code and account ownership, minimum access, environments, information handling and publication restrictions.

Delivery and acceptance

Deliverables, test scenarios, validation, documentation, support and change management. Licences and usage are assessed for the scope.
