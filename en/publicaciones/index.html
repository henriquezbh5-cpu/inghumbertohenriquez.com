How to read these publications

Clearly identified principles

Design proposals are presented as professional judgment. Claims drawn from third parties link to their sources.

Explicit scope

A conceptual example, a demonstration and a deployed system have different scopes. Each article identifies what it describes.

Available evidence

Public case studies link to demonstrations and documentation. Client metrics are not presented without evidence that can be published.

This section shares technical knowledge; it is not a catalog of peer-reviewed scientific publications.
